Booking

Choose a car from the collection and send an enquiry, or call the concierge desk directly. We confirm availability within the hour, hold the vehicle for 24 hours, and take payment only once your dates are locked in. The car is then prepared, detailed and delivered to the address you choose.

For weekends, weddings and event dates we recommend three to four weeks. Weekday bookings can often be arranged within 24 hours, subject to the vehicle being in the collection. If a specific model matters to you, tell us early and we will hold it.

Yes. Cancel or move your dates free of charge up to 72 hours before pick-up. Inside 72 hours we retain one day's rental; inside 24 hours, 50% of the booking. Modifications are always subject to availability.

Requirements

A valid driving licence held for at least two years, a government-issued photo ID, and a payment card in the primary driver's name. International licence holders should carry their passport alongside their licence.

25 for saloons, grand tourers and SUVs; 28 for supercars. Drivers between 21 and 24 may be considered for select models with an additional young-driver fee.

Yes. A refundable deposit is pre-authorised on your card at handover — $2,500 for saloons and SUVs, $5,000 for grand tourers and $10,000 for supercars. It is released in full once the vehicle is returned in the condition it left in.
